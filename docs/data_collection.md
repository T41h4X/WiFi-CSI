Time Interval Distribution:
- 0 to 10 ms   : ~65% of frames (high density burst traffic)

> [!WARNING]
> Because CSI packets are generated by incoming Wi-Fi frames from the router, the stream is not an isochronous clock. Downstream models must apply temporal interpolation or fixed-window resampling before sliding-window recognition.

## 3. Subcarrier Amplitude Behavior & Guard Band Anomalies
In an 802.11n 20 MHz OFDM physical layer, 64 subcarriers are defined:
* **Active Data Subcarriers**: Standard carriers like SC 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 36, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60 show mean amplitudes between 290 and 750 with healthy standard deviations (100 to 230), reflecting dynamic multipath sensitivity.
* **Central & Guard Nulls**: Subcarriers `[0, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35]` exhibit extreme or static anomalies:
  * Subcarrier 0 is the DC carrier.
  * Subcarrier 29 has a massive static mean amplitude of **34,915.89** (Std = 164.79).
  * Subcarriers 28, 31–35 show static plateaus (means between 3,200 and 15,100).
* **Research Conclusion**: These subcarriers represent guard bands, pilot tones, or internal firmware calibration constants. They must be masked out in preprocessing so they do not contaminate ML feature representations.
